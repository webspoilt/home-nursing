## 1. Executive Summary
This document provides complete instructions for operating, hosting, and managing the official digital web platform for **EarthCone Home Nursing**.

The website is engineered for maximum conversions, high-speed mobile loading, and direct WhatsApp customer capture to streamline patient onboarding in Bengaluru.

## 2. Platform Architecture
| Component | Technology | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **UI Framework** | HTML5, Tailwind CSS, Google Fonts | Responsive, high-conversion, medical-grade UX |
| **Icons** | Lucide Icons (CDN) | Lightweight, modern medical iconography |
| **Backend & API** | Node.js / Express / Vercel Serverless | Inquiry handling, persistence, and formatting |
| **Lead Engine** | WhatsApp Click-to-Chat Protocol | Instant lead dispatch to `+91 9931450495` |

---

## 3. Key Website Capabilities & Integrations

1. **Floating 24/7 WhatsApp Bubble:**
   - Always visible on mobile and desktop screens.
   - Pulsating animation to draw instant customer attention.

2. **Automated WhatsApp Booking Forms:**
   - **Hero Quick Form:** Captures name, phone, service, and Bangalore location in under 30 seconds.
   - **Comprehensive Inquiry Form:** Captures specific shift hours (12h, 24h, monthly) and patient medical notes.

3. **Pre-populated WhatsApp Service CTAs:**
   - Each service card contains a direct button that pre-fills the specific service title into WhatsApp for faster booking.

4. **Local SEO Coverage:**
   - Built-in metadata targeting BTM Layout, JP Nagar, HSR Layout, Koramangala, and Bannerghatta Road.
